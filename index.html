How YatraAI thinks

From your preferences to your journey

A simulated walk-through of how a recommendation engine would reason about a trip — this is the core academic concept behind YatraAI.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Understand

YatraAI reads your destination, duration, budget and interests as a structured traveller profile.

02 — MATCH

Match

Your interests are compared against each destination's tagged experiences to score how well it fits.

03 — OPTIMISE

Optimise

Cities and attractions are sequenced into a route that minimises backtracking and long transfers.

04 — PERSONALISE

Personalise

The final plan is adjusted for your pace, group size and budget before it's shown to you.

          About the project

          About YatraAI

          YatraAI is an educational prototype developed to demonstrate how artificial intelligence and digital technologies can transform tourism planning in India. It simulates an AI-powered recommendation system to show what modern, personalised travel-tech could look like for Indian tourism.

          Course: E-Tourism Skill Enhancement Course (SEC) Institution: University of Delhi

          Project theme

          Artificial Intelligence in E-Tourism

          The problem

          Travellers often need to search across multiple platforms to compare destinations, activities, budgets and itineraries — a fragmented, manual process with little personalisation.

          Proposed solution

          YatraAI demonstrates how AI-assisted personalisation can combine traveller preferences with destination information to create a single, organised, explainable travel plan.

          Project objectives

          • 🎯 Demonstrate AI applications in tourism
          • 🎯 Explore personalised travel planning
          • 🎯 Understand digital transformation in tourism
          • 🎯 Promote smart tourism practices
          • 🎯 Encourage sustainable, responsible travel
